SRD Games

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026
Publisher: SRD Games

This policy applies to all games published by SRD Games (Selim Sardarov), including Domino Borough, Flockfall and Lumen Wake, and to their Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and web builds, including copies distributed on Google Play, Steam or itch.io.

Short version. The games do not collect, store on any server, or share personal data. They have no accounts, no advertisements, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no network features of their own. Progress stays on your device.

What the games store, and where

Each game keeps data only in its private storage on your device (or, for desktop builds, in the game’s local user-data folder on your computer). These files are never sent to SRD Games or to anyone else by the game. Uninstalling an app, or deleting the desktop user-data folder, removes them.

Domino Borough

  • save.cfg — your progress: best scores and stars for each street, bricks, MY BOROUGH level and house skin, daily-task progress, buildings and charms you have already seen, first-launch flags, and settings (language, music and sound volume, graphics quality, screen shake, and vibration).
  • daily.cfg — your Daily Street record and streak.

Flockfall

A small save file on your device holds your progress (farm buildings, wool and coins, stars, daily tasks, login streak, Great Drive progress) and your settings (sound volume, tutorial state). Daily tasks and the login streak use your device’s date; that date is only compared on the device and is never sent anywhere.

Lumen Wake

Progress, save slots and settings stay in the game’s local user-data folder on your computer, or in the browser’s local storage for the web build. The game itself does not send this data anywhere.

What the games do not do

The games do not use accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, tracking, in-app purchases, or online multiplayer. They do not read your contacts, location, photos, microphone or advertising identifiers.

Network

The current Android builds of Domino Borough and Flockfall do not request the Internet permission and do not make network connections. Daily features are generated on your device from the date. They do not download anything.

The Windows, macOS and Linux builds are also played offline. They do not include Steam Cloud, online leaderboards, or other network features. In Domino Borough, a stub exists in the code for a possible future daily leaderboard; the current builds do not send that data.

Lumen Wake currently ships as desktop and web builds. The game itself has no accounts, advertising, analytics or network features of its own.

A web build is a set of static files. The website that hosts it may keep ordinary server logs (for example IP addresses and request times) under that host’s own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing.

Android permissions

Current Android builds do not request the Internet permission.

  • Domino Borough — Vibrate — short haptic buzzes when houses fall. You can turn this off in the game’s menu (Vibration: Off).

No other permissions are requested. If a future version needs a new permission, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Android backup

Android may include a game’s save files in your device’s own backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), so your progress can return when you set up a new phone. That backup is made by the Android system under your Google account and Google’s policies. You can control it in your phone’s settings (Settings → System → Backup). SRD Games does not receive this backup and cannot access it.

Children

The games do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. They do not contain accounts, chat, social features or advertising. A parent or guardian can delete a game’s local data by uninstalling it (or, for a desktop build, by deleting the game’s user-data folder).

Domino Borough is intended for players aged 13 and over. It includes some mild eerie imagery (an evening graveyard district with gravestones and a crypt). Flockfall is a casual game suitable for a general audience. Each game’s age rating, when listed, appears on its store page. Questions about this policy can be sent to the address below.

Third parties

The games contain no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. They are built with the open-source Godot Engine, which does not collect data in exported games.

If you download a game from Google Play, Steam, itch.io or another store, that store’s handling of your account, payments and downloads is covered by that store’s own privacy policy. SRD Games does not receive those account records through the game.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example because a future version adds an online feature — a new version will be posted at this address before that version is released, with a new effective date.
